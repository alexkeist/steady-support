Steady support

Steady offers illustrated movements and starter programs to help you find a place to begin.

Contact Alex Keist

Support email: steadyappsupport@gmail.com

For a technical problem, include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Avoid sending medical records or sensitive health details. Support is for app questions, not personal medical advice or emergencies.

Start a program

On Today, look for “Not sure where to start?”. Choose a program, review its movements and suitability, then select “Continue to session” and read the pre-session guidance.

Save and find movements

Tap the bookmark on a movement to save it. Open Movements and turn on “Saved only”, or use the saved-movements shortcut on Today. Tap a saved bookmark again to remove it.

Repeat a session

Use “Repeat last session” on Today or “Repeat session” in My progress. Older activity entries may not offer replay.

Delete activity

Open My progress, choose “Delete session” on the entry and confirm. This removes that entry from the app's current local history.
